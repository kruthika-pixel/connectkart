About ConnectKart

Building the future of community-driven commerce in India

🚀 Our Mission

Empowering Every Seller, Supporting Every Buyer

ConnectKart was built with a simple vision: create one seamless marketplace where customers can buy anything — from a plate of hot biryani to a refurbished laptop — from local restaurants, small shops, individual sellers, and even donate to communities in need.

We believe local businesses deserve the same digital infrastructure as large corporations, and that every community has potential for circular commerce through resale and donation.

Business Model & Revenue

Transparent commissions that make the platform sustainable

🍕

Restaurant Commission

ConnectKart earns 20% commission on every food order placed through the platform, paid by the restaurant partner.

Food Category
🛒

Shop Commission

Grocery stores and general shops pay 12% commission. Electronics sellers pay 8% on each successful sale.

Shop Category
⚙️

Platform Fee

A 3% platform/service fee (min ₹5, max ₹50) is charged to customers on each order to cover platform infrastructure costs.

All Categories
💡 All commission and fee rates are configurable via data/delivery.json — no code changes required to adjust the business model.

Platform Roadmap

Current state and upcoming production features

✅ Current (Frontend Prototype)

  • Responsive multi-page web application
  • Product browsing, search, and category filtering
  • Add to cart, quantity management, coupon codes
  • Configurable delivery charges and platform fee
  • Frontend checkout with payment method selection
  • Resale marketplace with seller listings
  • Donation platform (food, products, money)
  • Order confirmation with order ID generation
  • LocalStorage-based cart and order persistence

🔮 Upcoming (Production Phase)

  • Secure backend API (Node.js / Django)
  • Database integration (PostgreSQL / MongoDB)
  • User authentication and profiles
  • Real payment gateway (Razorpay / PayU)
  • Seller dashboard and admin panel
  • Live order tracking with delivery partner API
  • AI-powered recommendations (IBM watsonx Studio)
  • Push notifications and email alerts
  • Verified NGO donation with receipt generation
