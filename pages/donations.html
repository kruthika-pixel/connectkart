⚠️ Important Notice — Prototype Platform

This donation feature is currently a frontend prototype for demonstration purposes only.

Before this feature goes live in production, the following will be mandatory:

  • ✅ Verification and legal registration of all recipient NGOs / community organisations (80G / 12A certificates)
  • ✅ Secure payment gateway integration (Razorpay, PayU, or similar RBI-licensed gateway)
  • ✅ Proper donor authentication and KYC processes where legally required
  • ✅ Transparent reporting on how donations are utilised with receipts issued to donors
  • ✅ Full compliance with FCRA and applicable Indian charity regulations
  • ✅ Backend API, database, and auditing infrastructure

Do not make real financial transactions on this demo. No actual payments are processed. This page is for UI/UX demonstration only.

💰 Monetary Donation

⚠️ Demo Mode — No Real Payment

This is a prototype demonstration form only. No actual payment is processed. In the production version, this will integrate with a fully licensed and secure payment gateway (e.g., Razorpay or PayU), with proper UPI, card, and net banking flows compliant with RBI regulations. Donations will generate tax-deductible receipts only after NGO verification and legal compliance are complete.

Do NOT enter real card numbers, CVV, UPI pins, or banking credentials in this form.

🔒 Demo Only: No real payment gateway is active. In production, all transactions will be processed through RBI-approved, PCI-DSS compliant gateways. We will never store card details.
