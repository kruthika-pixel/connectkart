Quick FAQs

How do I track my order?

Order tracking will be available in the production version. For now, your order confirmation page shows your order ID and details.

How do I become a seller on ConnectKart?

Fill in the contact form with your business details and select "Seller / Restaurant Partnership" as your reason. Our team will reach out within 2 business days.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes. In production, all payments are processed by a certified, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. ConnectKart never stores card numbers, CVV, or UPI PINs.

How are donations used?

Donations are coordinated through verified NGO partners (pending regulatory verification for production launch). Full transparency reports will be published quarterly.
